# ALLSTAR-SCAPE — COMMUNITY / FORUM RESEARCH (web)
Compiled 2026-09-27. Markdown in a .txt file.

## Scope, method, legend
- Read: the still-live Allstar-Scape SMF forum (allstarscapeforums.smfforfree2.com) — all 18 threads of the "Bugs, glitches etc." board, the News/Recent Updates/Future Updates boards, most General Discussion threads, and ~60 forum keyword searches; Wayback captures of that forum (2008-09) and of Moparscape SMF threads (2010-12); Rune-Server threads (2008-2026); the moparisthebest.com Discourse mirror of old Moparscape posts; YouTube metadata and comments (2008-2013); a few fan sites.
- Could not read: RuneLocus forum (domain gone, not archived), Sythe (403), the Silabsoft forum (dead), Allstar's Photobucket screenshots (not archived), the original 2008 Moparscape V1 thread (only redirects archived), the AllstarScape Fandom wiki (402).
- Nothing was downloaded or executed. Section 7 only lists URLs.
- Everything is paraphrased; no long quotes. Thread titles are given as names.
- Confidence: [MULTI] = 2 or more independent sources agree; [SINGLE] = one source (can be one thread with several posters); [VAGUE] = indirect, ambiguous, or my inference.
- Version tags: LIVE-2008 = the original server allstar-scape.no-ip.biz as discussed on its forum (Apr 2008 to ~2010). V1 / V2 / V2.1 / V2.2 / V3 = source releases. Forks are named.
- YouTube comment dates are approximate (YouTube only shows relative age).
- [Sxx], [Rxx], [Mxx], [Yxx], [Oxx] = web sources in section 10. [Lx] = the local code inventory in scratchpad/inventory (NOT web; used only to cross-check).

## 0. Key takeaways
1. The blood-rune + Telekinetic Grab dupe is the most-cited Allstar exploit. It is described step by step in two separate ~2010 YouTube comments, and later forks list it as fixed. [MULTI]
2. The staff-zone rare box (the "puppet box") handed out 10 of each rare to anyone who reached or clicked it. This is confirmed by a 2010 hack video that lists the object IDs, a ~2010 comment, and the 2010 HolyPkerz fork's fix list. The IDs match object 10687 in the local v2 source. Ways in that people mention: cheat-client noclip, clicking far on the minimap before the zone walls spawn, and climbing a shop-area ladder to the floor above. [MULTI]
3. More dupes: trade accept-then-decline timing (June 2008), self-trade through a cheat client (2010), and alching one item over and over (2009; a comment says it only worked on the Allstar source). [MULTI]
4. Abuse items: the 'perfect' ring (players called them "prings") and the glowing dagger were banned in PK (2009-2010). "Allstar's Hammer" was a renamed meat tenderiser and a fan favourite (90 max hit). [MULTI]
5. ::noclip was a trap command that autobanned whoever typed it. [MULTI]
6. Lineage: Allstar said V1 was based on BrettScape V3, but most other people call it a Project Czar derivative. V2.1 and V2.2 ("fixed") were made by purez4life (Moparscape) and posted on Rune-Server in June 2008. A V3 followed in 2009, plus dozens of forks. [MULTI]
7. The 2008 server had no client of its own. Players were sent to the Silabsoft client, because the Moparscape 3.2 client froze on Silab (508-era) items. [MULTI]

## 1. BUGS, GLITCHES, DUPES & EXPLOITS

### 1A. Item duplication

1A-1 Blood-rune bank-withdraw stall + Telekinetic Grab — [MULTI]
- Steps as told around 2010 (OwnerPizza, commenting on a Sept 2009 "allstar-source" dupe video [Y6]):
  1. Carry law and air runes (the cost of telegrab).
  2. Drop the item you want to copy.
  3. Open the bank and withdraw-all on a big stack of blood runes.
  4. While the withdrawal is still running, keep casting Telekinetic Grab on the dropped item. Each cast gives another copy until the withdrawal finishes.
  5. Result: a full inventory of the item.
- A second ~2010 comment (Bulkii [Y7]) gives the same steps, notes telegrab needs 33 Magic, and ties the trick to the Allstar source.
- Corroboration:
  - Rune-Server 2014: a player remembers the "blood rune item dupe", and another asks whether the telegrab dupe will still work in a remake [R9].
  - iScape changelog, 2015: "blood rune lags" are listed among fixed Allstar problems, while telegrab was kept [R8].
  - HolyPkerz, 2010: stopped telegrab dupes by removing law runes from the server entirely [M1].
  - Paradise, 2013: lists "Telegrab Dupe" as fixed [R6].
  - A generic 2017 article (not Allstar-specific) uses 5,000 blood runes and says the withdrawal happens bit by bit over several seconds [O7].
- Refinement: the stall comes from withdrawing a huge stack. [VAGUE] In the local v2, ::starter gives 20,000 of every rune, including blood, law and air [L1/L3], so every new account had the materials.

1A-2 Telegrab on items you can't pick up — [SINGLE]
- A ~2011 comment on Robgob's video [Y5] passes on a tip: pickup fails on a spawned or unpickupable ground item, but Telekinetic Grab still takes it.
- Not found anywhere: the specific "stale telegrab from the right-click menu on PK drops" variant.

1A-3 Trade window accept/decline dupe — [MULTI] that trade dupes existed; [SINGLE] for the exact method
- 2008-06-07 [S29]: accept the trade, then decline it with the right timing, and the items are duplicated. A second poster confirmed it on 06-17. Staff never answered in the thread.
- Later fix lists:
  - HolyPkerz 2010: anti trade dupe [M1].
  - Paradise 2013: trade dupe fixed, including the cheat-client version [R6].
  - iScape 2015: all trade bugs fixed [R8].
- A ~2010 commenter also claims he could steal items in trade without scamming, but gives no method [Y6]. [VAGUE]

1A-4 Self-trade dupe (cheat client) — [SINGLE]
- Robgob's 2010 video "Hacking Allstar based rsps" [Y5] shows a dupe. His fix includes blocking trades with your own player ID, so the dupe was trading yourself through a cheat client (he says it was Bomb's cheat client).
- Servers he says it worked on: pkzscapezzz, emov3, cashscape, and afgscape (in the comments).

1A-5 Alch dupe (high-alch one item many times) — [MULTI]
- Aug 2009 video "silab DUPE" [Y7]: a Silab cheat for alching one item many times. A ~2010 comment says it works only on the Allstar source.
- Paradise 2013 lists "Alch Dupe" as fixed [R6].
- Follow-up posts from 2019-2021 on the same thread [R6 p3]:
  - It only stopped because a client UID check blocked unofficial clients, and the UID can be bypassed.
  - Robgob: the real fix is to check, in the alch packet handler, that the player actually holds the item.
- 2008 mod applications promise to warn people about alching [S69]. [VAGUE]

1A-6 Rares box in the Mod/Admin ("staff") zone — [MULTI]
- Robgob 2010 [Y5]:
  - Shows how to get into the "puppet box" that was left full of good items.
  - Lists IDs 10687, 10691, 10692, 10693 and 10695.
  - Fix: add a player-rights check to the object clicks.
- ~2010 comment by zpsh1212 [Y6]:
  - Use a noclip cheat client to walk into the admin-only zone.
  - Mass-click the chests; each click gives 10 of every rare.
  - Sell them noted, about 5m each.
- HolyPkerz 2010 fix list [M1]: the staff-zone rare box was changed so only Mod+ can take the rares.
- Background:
  - V1 (May 2008) has a Mod/Admin Zone reached with ::az [R1].
  - June 2008 forum posts mention ::az and a "Mod/Admin portal" [S69].
  - A 2014 remake re-added the "puppet box" [R9].
- Local cross-check [L2]:
  - Object 10687 ("Puppet torsos") sits at 3285,2770 in the Sophanem Mod zone.
  - It gives 10 noted of each partyhat colour, the santa hat and each h'ween mask.
  - Objects 10691-10693 and 10695 also hand out rares but are not placed in the world.
  - This is exactly Robgob's ID list. It is strong evidence that the local v2 is the same Allstar build these 2009-2010 exploits targeted.

### 1D. PK / combat exploits and abuse
- PK box edge + Vengeance — [SINGLE thread], 2008-04-28 [S16]
  - Standing on the edge of the PK line let you hit people outside with vengeance while they couldn't hit you back.
  - Staff answer: verbal warning, then kick, then ban. Fatality added that staff abusing it should be reported with screenshots.
- Invisibility — [MULTI]
  - 2008-06-12 [S35]: wearing armadyl legs makes you invisible.
  - Other 2008 posts complain about invisible PKers, and about an admin who killed people while invisible and teleported them [S69].
  - Paradise 2013 lists a "go invisible" glitch as fixed [R6].
  - Local v2: a Mod-Allstar-only ::hide makes you invisible [L1]. That may explain some staff cases. [VAGUE]
- Staff teleporting players into the Wilderness and killing them — [SINGLE], 2008-07-31 [S46]
  - Accounts "D D S P E C" and "D D S P 3 C" teleported players into the wild and killed them for their items. The same post says they were handing out staff ranks.
  - Possibly related: a 2008 YouTube channel "ddspecs" featuring Mod Allstar and "coder danny" [Y15], and later dds-specz / crayolascape Allstar servers [Y17, Y18]. [VAGUE]
  - Local v2 hard-codes an account "D D 3" with dev and kick powers [L1]. [VAGUE]
- 'Perfect' ring / glowing dagger abuse in PK — [MULTI]
  - 2009-01-29: a player complains about abusers with "prings" [S62].
  - 2009-01-30: an admin application promises not to "perfect ring" people [S63].
  - 2010-06-22: an admin application rule — no perfect ring or glowing dagger in PK [S68].
  - What either item actually did is not described anywhere.
- Protection prayers — [VAGUE]
  - The rules of a Hamachi-hosted copy punish "prayer" with a black mark (and "cart" with a 3-day ban) [O1].
  - Local v2: any protection prayer makes you immune to player melee and range [L3]. That probably explains the rule.
- NPCs mostly don't fight back on Allstars (iScape 2015 [R8]) [SINGLE]. On 2009-01-02 a player was surprised when monsters suddenly hit back hard (repeated 24s) [S60]. [SINGLE]
- Shopkeepers could be killed with magic — [VAGUE/MULTI]
  - A 2014 remake commenter asks to keep the shop killers killable with mage [R9].
  - Paradise 2013 stopped magic being cast on shopkeepers, and stopped players attacking themselves [R6].
- Every weapon shows a special-attack bar (Allstar Reborn 2013 [R10]). [SINGLE]

### 1G. Stability and security weaknesses
- A 100k socket buffer caused crashes [R2, R3; Dec 2008]. [MULTI]
- Capacity claims disagree:
  - it crashes at 10 players vs. 50 players on a Pentium 4 [M4];
  - 400 bots, but very laggy [Y13, M4];
  - "would have crashed at about 50" [R10].
- No flood protection:
  - An admin threatened to SYI (connection-flood) leechers [S50].
  - The server was hit by spam/DoS attacks in Sept 2008 [S51].
  - Forks later added 1 connection per IP [M1, R6].
  - Local v2: the per-IP limit never works because of a String == comparison [L3].
- Accounts or stats reset on disconnect — [MULTI]: FizzerX 2021 [R13], Paradise 2013 (random resets) [R6], a 2023 question to Allstarlegends [R15], and a silab-era memory of rollbacks on disconnect [R23].
- Cheating was easy:
  - Allstar was called very easy to cheat with Cheat Engine or dupes [R13, 2021].
  - Cheat clients existed (Robgob / Bomb's) [Y5], and later forks added client UID checks [R6].
  - Local v2 logs UID -74352552 as a "XeroScape cheat client" [L3]. That explains the XeroScape name in the source.

### 1H. What forks said they fixed (a useful "known Allstar bugs" checklist)
- HolyPkerz 2010 [M1]:

  - anti trade and telegrab dupe (law runes removed);
  - ogre bow glitch;
  - Barrows requirement glitch;

  - sharks and mantas cookable;
  - main rares removed from shops and monster drops;
  - meat tenderiser and godswords removed from shops (tenderiser became a rare KBD drop);

  - anti-SYI;
  - saves on crash;
  - trade dupe, including cheat clients;
  - "many other dupes";
  - invisibility glitch;
  - players attacking themselves;
  - magic on shopkeepers;
  - NPC HP reroll;
  - telegrab dupe;
  - alch dupe;
  - random account resets (NOT fixed).
- iScape 2015 [R8]:
  - trade bugs, drop glitches, cheat-client exploits and blood-rune lags;
  - smithing level requirements;
  - working NPC combat.
